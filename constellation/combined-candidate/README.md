# Combined Constellation candidate

This neutral candidate combines the accepted operational spine and Workbench. It is prepared for owner review; human stranger-operability qualification and Beta Candidate 1 publication have not occurred. Exact artifact identities and the focused integration record accompany the download.

Start with [installation](INSTALLATION.md), [operation](OPERATIONS.md), and [day two](DAY-TWO-RUNBOOK.md). [Support](SUPPORT.md) states the scope. The prepared [human exercise](HUMAN-OPERABILITY.md) is later, separately scheduled beta evidence. The [integration contract](WORKBENCH-INTEGRATION-CONTRACT.md) identifies the installed interfaces for integrators.

Operational EDA—EDA for operations—applies Electronic Design Automation-like composition, analysis, verification, qualification and automation to operating systems and services: model what exists, instrument what matters, establish current evidence, diagnose within explicit bounds, govern changes and verify independent postconditions. Workbench is its human-facing surface; the operational spine describes the underlying composition. This is a developing architecture and product direction. Historical operational_ecad CLI/schema identifiers remain for traceability and compatibility.
