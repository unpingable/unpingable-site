## Claim and formalization boundary

Each included row requires exact source, package/executable and meaningful target evidence. A source link, schema match, copied fixture, process exit, metrics graph or historical pass cannot establish a current installed generation. Native observation, support, attention, permission, custody, accounting and postcondition records remain separate. The fresh combined target is accepted only by its independent acceptance owner after required results and gaps are reviewed.

Formalization consideration: this matrix claims finite composition and explicit exclusions, not new runtime behavior or cross-generation compatibility. Exact source/package dependency inspection and bounded existing lifecycle tests are sufficient for the matrix. Unknowns are fresh composed target results, held Workbench dependencies and any independently deployed observability services. The spine integration owner decides inclusion and acceptance; no universal compatibility model is introduced.
