Compose responsibilities, not a monolith

Choose the smallest set of components that owns the decisions and records your workflow actually needs. Passing data between tools does not transfer authority.

Interfaces and caller boundaries

These are supported component interfaces, not a universal cross-component protocol. A caller may name input and identity; it does not thereby obtain a permission, currentness fact, or ownership of retained records.

There is no supported instruction here to inspect another owner’s database or to substitute an opaque locator for an owner identity. Keep credentials, private prompts, and whole databases out of diagnostics; use the documented component route and retain only the minimal redacted evidence needed to explain a refusal or unavailable source.

Choosing and recovering

Need to tell a human when something fails? Read Human notifications before migrating Classic notification hooks. The bounded Slack/Discord adapter and local receipt replay are implemented; actual channel delivery and application deployment remain unverified. A finite saved-check recurrence and local-inbox composition is now public-only qualified. Retained evidence and an inspection UI are not delivery.

Need to check a local SQLite source and inspect the result later? NQ’s saved-check example runs without a model, daemon, or private prerequisite. Its public-only reproduction covers exact replay, stale-input refusal, and a retained condition view that keeps outcome, source freshness, local-read evidence and maintenance separate. Later maintenance declarations cannot rewrite an earlier view. This is an experimental local capability, not a released multi-component profile or a complete recurring-monitoring migration.

Use Operations for retained-state/restart guidance and Troubleshooting for a minimal report. In an uncertain governed attempt, inspect and reconcile the retained attempt first; do not repeat mechanics merely to obtain a tidier answer. Cancellation and expiry must come from the selected component contract—there is no suite-wide cancel or renewal guarantee.

Unsupported combinations

  • Federation is not a supported profile. A shared HTTP or MCP transport does not establish matching plan, evidence, review, or authority semantics.
  • The deprecated Python governor and classic NQ are not drop-in implementations of the successor AG/NQ contracts.
  • Maude's design workspace and AG-hosted Phosphor inspection do not share a plan store. Navigation between them does not supply handoff or execution.
  • Porter's command transport alone does not supply an arbitrary Docket executor, task-specific NQ profile, or Nightshift composition.
  • General ECAD/design-flow and arbitrary campaign execution remain outside the advertised supported recipes.
