External interfaces

Plans and proposals
Maude produces checked, locked plan material. A proposal remains candidate input until an owning admission boundary accepts it.
Observation
Nightshift owns currentness and recurrence records. NQ derives a bounded disposition from exact evidence. Neither grants authority.
Authorization
Constellation AG decides whether exact work may proceed and records one-use consumption. Integrators must not infer permission from a passing checker or successful prior run.
Execution custody
Docket owns attempt identity, executor binding, settlement, and reconciliation. An executor owns its mechanics and evidence. Porter can be such a transport ingredient only through an explicitly supported adapter; it does not become Docket by returning a transcript.
Read surfaces
Phosphor and Spine read selected owner records. Their indexes and pages do not modify those records or decide their meaning.

Examples and caller boundaries

After setup, use Operations for retained state and restart guidance, or Troubleshooting for inspection and a minimal issue report.

The integration recipes distinguish commands you can run from source today from a connected workflow that still lacks a public-only distribution. Each recipe names its input substitutions and the point at which the caller remains responsible.

Compatibility is explicit. Repository names, shared vocabulary, or individually passing tests do not establish that arbitrary revisions compose. Follow the revision and interface constraints in each component’s own public guide.

Unsupported compositions

  • Federation is not a supported profile. A shared HTTP or MCP transport does not establish matching plan, evidence, review, or authority semantics.
  • The deprecated Python governor and classic NQ are not drop-in implementations of the successor AG/NQ contracts.
  • Maude's design workspace and AG-hosted Phosphor inspection do not share a plan store. Navigation between them does not supply handoff or execution.
  • Porter's command transport alone does not supply an arbitrary Docket executor, task-specific NQ profile, or Nightshift composition.
  • General ECAD/design-flow and arbitrary campaign execution remain outside the advertised supported recipes.
